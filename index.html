What we make.

Want to learn more or work with us? Start a conversation.

MKM Studio is a software and game development studio creating digital experiences for mobile and PC, including games, social platforms and AI-powered product concepts.

Star City RP is an open-world role-play game project for mobile, centered on player-created stories and multiplayer interaction.

They are MKM Studio products: AFBOOK helps people connect and share; MKM AI explores intelligent assistance; AI CALL focuses on voice communication; and MKM Pocket helps users record income, expenses, budgets and debts.
